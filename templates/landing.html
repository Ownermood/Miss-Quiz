What it does in your group

Your group studies while it chats.

Add the bot, make it admin, and the group turns into a daily practice room. Nobody has to run anything.

A fresh quiz every hour

Miss Quiz posts an exam-style question every 60 minutes. When the next one arrives, the old one is removed, so the chat stays clean.

  • Lands in the topic where people are talking
  • Scores add up on the group leaderboard

Battle a friend, one on one

Challenge someone in the group or send an invite in DM. Same questions, same timer. Faster right answer wins the tie.

  • Classic, Quick or Marathon in one tap
  • Wins and losses are saved to your profile

Run a full timed quiz

Admins start a session with /startquiz. Timed questions, a leaderboard after every 10, and pause, skip or stop whenever you need.

  • Picks up where it left off after a restart
  • Use your own quiz or 10, 20 or 30 from the bot

Any file becomes a quiz

Send a PDF, Word, Excel or text file, or forward quiz polls. Miss Quiz reads the questions, options and answer key and builds your quiz.

  • Coaching PDFs with answer keys at the end work too
  • Tells you exactly which questions it skipped and why
